## Verdict

The 2025 Alarm-rate dip (-1.16 pp from 2024) is **not** explained by any single specialty pulling back. Biggest mix shift: **Ophthalmology** -5.5 pp, counter-balanced by **Medical Informatics / Digital Health** +3.9 pp. Most likely interpretation: the 2025 'dip' is a **publication-lag / denominator-growth artifact** — the absolute Alarm count rose but the total corpus (denominator) grew faster as Cautious Optimism papers expanded with the field. Partial-year 2026 already shows Alarm rate recovering.
