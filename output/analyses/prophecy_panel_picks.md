# Prophecy panel — top 3 picks per year, with reasoning

**Source:** filtered from `prophecy_panel_candidates_2021_2023.csv` (92 records: 28 / 25 / 39).
**Selection criteria:** (1) specific testable claim (year/role/benchmark), (2) transformative framing ("revolutionize / paradigm shift / transform / will"), (3) now-checkable against 2024–2026 reality, (4) venue weight, (5) clinical-specialty diversity across the 9 picks.

**Specialty coverage across the 9 picks:** Med Education, Med Informatics, Dermatology, Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Surgery, Ob/Gyn, Oncology, Radiology — 9 distinct fields.

## 2021 — pre-ChatGPT broad-strokes era

### Pick 1.1 — Acad Med, "ML: The Next Paradigm Shift in Medical Education"

- **PMID:** 33496428 | **Journal:** Academic Medicine (AAMC's journal) | **Specialty:** Medical Education
- **Headline claim:** ML literacy will become an essential component of medical curricula, "similar to the evidence-based medicine (EBM) movement of the 1990s."
- **Why this is a strong prophecy candidate:** explicit historical analog (EBM took ~10 years to fully integrate); paragraph predicts the same arc for ML. Falsifiable: by 2026, is ML literacy standard in MD curricula?
- **2026 reality-check angle:** AAMC's own AI competency framework + LCME accreditation status; AAMC competencies for AI literacy were drafted in 2024–2025. Partially borne out — but uneven.

### Pick 1.2 — JAMA, "Next-Generation AI for Diagnosis: From Predicting Diagnostic Labels to 'Wayfinding'"

- **PMID:** 34882190 | **Journal:** JAMA | **Specialty:** Medical Informatics
- **Headline claim:** AI diagnosis will move beyond classification toward "wayfinding" — guiding clinicians through diagnostic reasoning steps.
- **Why this is a strong prophecy candidate:** explicit conceptual reframing (label → reasoning) and JAMA imprimatur. Extremely quotable.
- **2026 reality-check angle:** Med-PaLM 2, GPT-4 differential-diagnosis literature, MEDITRON, AMIE (Google's diagnostic AI) — the "wayfinding" framing is partially realized in 2024–25 LLM reasoning studies. Quote against Med-PaLM 2 NEJM paper, AMIE 2024.

### Pick 1.3 — Scientific Reports, "AI outperformed every dermatologist in dermoscopic melanoma diagnosis"

- **PMID:** 34471174 | **Journal:** Scientific Reports (Nature) | **Specialty:** Dermatology
- **Headline claim:** AI beat every dermatologist on dermoscopic melanoma with an optimized deep-CNN.
- **Why this is a strong prophecy candidate:** concrete benchmark + outperformance claim. Easily compared against 2024–2026 dermatology AI deployment.
- **2026 reality-check angle:** has dermatology AI reached routine clinical use? Spoiler — still mostly research-stage, FDA-cleared tools exist but uptake limited. This is the classic "AI beats specialists on benchmark, fails to deploy at scale" prediction.

## 2022 — late-pre-ChatGPT / GPT-3 era

### Pick 2.1 — Frontiers in Endocrinology, "Cushing's Disease Management: Glimpse Into 2051"

- **PMID:** 35872988 | **Journal:** Frontiers in Endocrinology | **Specialty:** Internal Medicine
- **Headline claim:** "Endocrinology care will be **completely reinvented** in the Digital Age." Format is a fictional 2051 patient narrative.
- **Why this is a strong prophecy candidate:** unique format (a short story) + explicit "completely reinvented" language. The 2051 horizon is too far for now-check, but the underlying trajectory is checkable in 2026.
- **2026 reality-check angle:** is endocrinology care discernibly different from 2022 in any structural way? (Probably no — same diagnostic algorithms, same medication management workflows.)

### Pick 2.2 — International Journal of Cardiology, "AI vs physicians on STEMI ECG"

- **PMID:** 35691440 | **Journal:** International Journal of Cardiology | **Specialty:** Cardiology
- **Headline claim:** Smartphone-camera AI **outperforms 7 emergency physicians** on STEMI detection from ECG images.
- **Why this is a strong prophecy candidate:** concrete head-to-head benchmark, smartphone-AI scenario directly testable.
- **2026 reality-check angle:** smartphone-camera ECG screening at scale — Apple Watch ECG, AliveCor, FDA-cleared apps. Has the "outperformance" translated into deployed clinical pathways or stayed in research? Strong "benchmark vs deployment" prophecy.

### Pick 2.3 — JBJS Reviews, "Understanding AI and Predictive Analytics in Orthopedics"

- **PMID:** 35302963 | **Journal:** JBJS Reviews | **Specialty:** Surgery (orthopedic)
- **Headline claim:** "Machine learning will soon become **critical in the day-to-day operations** of orthopaedic practice."
- **Why this is a strong prophecy candidate:** concrete operational claim — day-to-day operations, not research. Quotable and testable.
- **2026 reality-check angle:** is ML actually in day-to-day orthopedic practice in 2026? (Mostly no — pre-op planning tools exist but not "critical" to operations.)

## 2023 — post-ChatGPT enthusiasm peak

### Pick 3.1 — AJOG, "ChatGPT outscored human candidates in a virtual OSCE in Ob/Gyn"

- **PMID:** 37088277 | **Journal:** American Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology | **Specialty:** Ob/Gyn
- **Headline claim:** ChatGPT outscored humans on a mock virtual OSCE in obstetrics and gynecology.
- **Why this is a strong prophecy candidate:** concrete benchmark on professional examination; one of the earliest "ChatGPT passes [exam]" papers in clinical literature.
- **2026 reality-check angle:** has the OSCE-passing capability translated into anything clinical, or is it still benchmark-only? Strong "passes test, doesn't change practice" exemplar.

### Pick 3.2 — Cancer Biother & Radiopharm, "Cancer Care by Committee to be **Superseded** by AI"

- **PMID:** 37366774 | **Journal:** Cancer Biotherapy & Radiopharmaceuticals | **Specialty:** Oncology
- **Headline claim:** Multidisciplinary tumor boards (an institutional standard since the 1990s) will be **superseded** by personal physician–patient partnerships informed by AI.
- **Why this is a strong prophecy candidate:** bold, falsifiable institutional-disruption claim. Predicts the obsolescence of an established clinical workflow.
- **2026 reality-check angle:** are MTBs still the standard in oncology in 2026? (Yes — emphatically.) High-payoff "wildly wrong" prophecy.

### Pick 3.3 — Radiology, "Context-based Chatbot **Surpasses Trained Radiologists** on ACR Appropriateness Guidelines"

- **PMID:** 37489981 | **Journal:** Radiology (RSNA) | **Specialty:** Radiology
- **Headline claim:** A custom chatbot surpasses trained radiologists and generic ChatGPT on ACR appropriateness criteria interpretation.
- **Why this is a strong prophecy candidate:** concrete head-to-head benchmark + high venue (Radiology). Tests the "LLMs beat specialists on guideline tasks" claim against 2024–26 deployment reality.
- **2026 reality-check angle:** has guideline-following chatbots become deployed in radiology decision-support? Limited adoption — most radiology AI is still image-classification, not guideline-reasoning.

## Narrative arc the 9 picks tell

- **2021 — broad-strokes pre-ChatGPT era:** medicine integrates AI like it integrated EBM (Med Ed); diagnosis reasoning moves "beyond labels" (JAMA); benchmark wins on melanoma (Derm).
- **2022 — late-pre-ChatGPT, GPT-3 era:** specialty narratives reach futurism (Cushing's-2051); smartphone-AI claims outperform clinicians (STEMI); ML is "soon" critical to day-to-day practice (orthopedics).
- **2023 — post-ChatGPT enthusiasm peak:** LLMs pass professional exams (OSCE); institutional workflows declared obsolete (tumor boards); custom chatbots beat specialists on guidelines (Radiology).

Each year tier shows the progression: 2021 (analogies and future-state claims) → 2022 (specific benchmarks and "soon" framing) → 2023 (exam passing, institutional disruption, head-to-head LLM-vs-specialist).
