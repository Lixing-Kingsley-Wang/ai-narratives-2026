## General observations (Claude's read; Kingsley to refine)

- **Specialty shift:** 2021 Advocacy concentrated in Medical Informatics / Digital Health, Oncology, Neurology / Neuroscience; 2026 survivors concentrated in Medical Informatics / Digital Health, Dentistry, Multidisciplinary / Other.
- **Geographic shift:** 2021 spread across 8 regions; 2026 across 5 regions.
- **Predictive-claim fraction:** 2021 28/31 = 90% forward-looking predictions; 2026 16/17 = 94%.

**Interpretation seed for the Commentary** (Kingsley to refine):

The Advocacy stance has not disappeared but has narrowed substantially. Where 2021 saw broad-strokes forward-looking claims ("AI will revolutionize X") across multiple specialties, 2026 Advocacy survives mainly as field-specific endorsements with concrete clinical anchors. This convergence with Cautious Optimism — rather than disappearance — is consistent with the broader narrative of medicine absorbing AI into its existing measured-language conventions, rather than rejecting AI outright.
