Trend statistics: Spearman ρ of the annual shares on year with **exact** two-sided permutation P (all 5! = 120 orderings; with 5 points the smallest attainable two-sided P is 2/120 ≈ .017), and record-level unadjusted logistic regression of the outcome on publication year (continuous, centred on the record-level mean year of the window), OR per year with Wald 95% CI and Wald P (likelihood-ratio P in the csv). No covariates.

Analysis set sizes (2021–2025) are the `records` column below; e.g. stance 14,058 of 16,747 records dated 2021–2026.
