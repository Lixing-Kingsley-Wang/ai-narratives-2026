**Annual count and prevalence share of the ten concern themes, 2021-2025.** Grey bars (left axis): number of critical records assigned the theme each year (printed above each bar). Blue line (right axis): prevalence among critical records (printed, %), drawn in the upper part of each panel on its own scale. Two measures are shown per panel. Denominator per year: the critical records of that year (Figure 2). Themes were multilabel. Annual shares with 95% CIs are in Figure 2 (headline themes) and {{F:other_themes_fig}} (other themes); pooled shares are in {{T:themes}}. Critical stance was Alarm or Caution. Years are shown as two digits (21 = 2021).
