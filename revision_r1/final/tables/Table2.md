Logistic regression of critical stance (Alarm or Caution) on publication year (continuous, centred on 2023.856), genre, publication type, specialty and first-author region; n=14,058; events=4283; 2278 journal clusters; cluster-robust (journal) SEs.

Critical stance was Alarm or Caution. Unadjusted OR: one-predictor logistic model with the same cluster-robust SEs. Adjusted OR: all covariates in the table entered together. P values are for the adjusted OR (Wald, cluster-robust). AMP: average marginal predicted probability of critical stance with all records set to that level and other covariates at their observed values; 95% CI by the delta method on the logit scale with the cluster-robust covariance.

Reference levels: discourse (genre), Research Article (publication type), Medical Informatics / Digital Health (specialty), North America (region); chosen as the most frequent level. Region 'Unknown' (affiliation not resolvable) and specialty 'Unassigned' (classification FAILED) are kept as levels so that no record is dropped.

Standard errors are cluster-robust by journal (normalised SJR-matching title; 2278 clusters).

Not included: model type (failure-mechanism axis), because it is defined only for critical records and therefore cannot predict critical stance; and FDA cleared-device count, because it is an 18-unit category-level (ecological) variable that is fully determined by specialty and would be collinear with the specialty terms.

Sensitivity models (categorical year, adding January-April 2026, journal fixed effects, first-author country instead of region), the ordinal model with its proportional-odds check and the Advocacy model are in Multimedia Appendix 3.
