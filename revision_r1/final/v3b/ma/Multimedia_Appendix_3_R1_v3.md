# Multimedia Appendix 3. Supplementary Figures

![Figure S1](units/figures/country_map_20.png)

**Figure S1. Critical rate by country of first authorship at the 20-record threshold, 2021-2025.** As Figure 5, but including all 42 countries with at least 20 first-author records; the 10 countries with 20 to 49 records have a dashed outline and are low-confidence estimates. Bubble area is proportional to the number of records; fill colour and the printed value give the critical rate (%). Europe is enlarged in the inset, where its values are printed; some low-count countries overlap at map scale and their values are in Multimedia Appendix 2, Table S27. Country indexes publication discourse, not national attitudes, research quality or AI capability. Critical stance was Alarm or Caution. Robinson projection.

![Figure S2](units/figures/forward_validation_sankey.png)

**Figure S2. Exploratory forward-validation of early Advocacy predictions (Advocacy only).** The flow diagram shows 74 papers, 78 claims from Advocacy records published in 2021-2023, grouped by falsifiability tier and adjudicated against evidence published from 2024 to April 2026. Node height and ribbon width represent claim counts. A single rater assigned each claim as borne out, partially borne out, not borne out, or too early/unfalsifiable; 'not borne out' required positive contrary evidence and corpus silence was classified as too early/unfalsifiable. The evidence window extends to April 2026 by design. The tier by verdict matrix is in Multimedia Appendix 2, Table S8. The analysis was exploratory, Advocacy-only, single-rater and retrieval-dependent; it does not adjudicate AI capability or whether critical stances were correct. Critical stance was Alarm or Caution; favourable stance was Cautious Optimism or Advocacy; this figure concerns Advocacy records only.

![Figure S4](units/figures/terms_fig.png)
