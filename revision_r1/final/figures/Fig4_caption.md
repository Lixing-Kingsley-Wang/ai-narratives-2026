**Figure 4. Critical stance by specialty or domain category and FDA cleared-device count, 2021-2025.** (a) Observed critical rate (filled circles) and adjusted average marginal probability of critical stance (open diamonds) for the 18 categories among 14,048 records with a valid specialty label, sorted by the observed rate. Observed CIs are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals (1000 joint resamples, seed 42); adjusted values come from the multivariable logistic model in Table 2 (year, genre, publication type, specialty and region; journal-clustered SEs; delta-method 95% CI on the logit scale). (b) Observed critical rate against the number of FDA-cleared AI/ML-enabled devices in the review panels mapped to each category (log scale). The 7 structural-zero categories (no mapped FDA panel; not an absence of clinical AI) are drawn as hollow markers at 0.5. No fitted line is shown. Labels give the critical rate (%) and device count. Spearman correlation, all 18 categories: ρ=-0.61 (two-sided P=.007; one-sided P=.004; permutation one-sided P=.005); 11 categories with a mapped panel: ρ=-0.58 (two-sided P=.062; one-sided P=.031; permutation one-sided P=.036). This is an exploratory ecological association across 18 category-level units. Critical stance was Alarm or Caution.
