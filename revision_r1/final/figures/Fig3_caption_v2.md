**Figure 3. Failure mechanism among critical records, 2021-2025.** (a) Annual failure-mode composition among 1775 hallucination-gated, abstract-present critical records (critical records assigned the hallucination/errors theme). Each bar sums to 100%; the table under the bars gives every within-year percentage (segments of 6% or more are also labelled in the bar), and n above each bar gives the annual count. Confabulation is reported as fabrication and misclassification as factual error. (b) Annual generative-model share (generative or both) and fabrication share (confabulation or both) among all 3906 abstract-present critical records; points show annual estimates and whiskers 95% percentile bootstrap CIs (1000 joint resamples, seed 42). Critical stance was Alarm or Caution. Record-level logistic regression on publication year (unadjusted; journal cluster-robust SEs): generative-model share OR 2.026 per year (95% CI 1.892-2.170), P<.001; fabrication share OR 1.552 per year (95% CI 1.416-1.701), P<.001; fabrication-involved share among hallucination-gated records OR 1.347 per year (95% CI 1.197-1.515), P<.001. Failure-mode agreement with human coding was moderate (Cohen κ 0.57 (95% CI 0.46-0.69)) and the classifier assigned fabrication more often than the human coder (20 vs 1 disagreements), so fabrication shares are an upper bound.
