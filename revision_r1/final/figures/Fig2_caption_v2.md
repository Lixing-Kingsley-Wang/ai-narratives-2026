**Figure 2. Annual evaluative stance and headline concern themes, 2021-2025.** (a) Annual share of each stance category among 14,058 records with a valid stance (per-year n: 2021 1063, 2022 1343, 2023 2054, 2024 3700, 2025 5898). (b) Annual prevalence of the four headline concern themes among 4283 critical records (per-year n: 2021 270, 2022 333, 2023 624, 2024 1228, 2025 1828); themes were multilabel (one to three per record), so prevalences do not sum to 100%. Critical stance was Alarm or Caution; favourable stance was Cautious Optimism or Advocacy. Points show annual estimates and whiskers 95% percentile bootstrap CIs (1000 joint resamples of records, seed 42); printed values are the point estimates (%). Record-level logistic regression of the outcome on publication year (unadjusted; journal cluster-robust SEs): critical OR 1.077 per year (95% CI 1.038-1.116), P<.001; Advocacy OR 0.793 per year (95% CI 0.720-0.873), P<.001; Patient safety OR 1.055 per year (95% CI 0.995-1.118), P=.071; Hallucination / errors OR 1.367 per year (95% CI 1.289-1.450), P<.001; Governance / regulation OR 0.765 per year (95% CI 0.719-0.814), P<.001; Ethics & bias OR 0.915 per year (95% CI 0.861-0.972), P=.004. Spearman correlation of the five annual values with year (exact permutation P): Alarm ρ=+0.80 (P=.133); Caution ρ=+0.60 (P=.350); Neutral ρ=-0.90 (P=.083); Cautious Optimism ρ=-0.60 (P=.350); Advocacy ρ=-1.00 (P=.017).
