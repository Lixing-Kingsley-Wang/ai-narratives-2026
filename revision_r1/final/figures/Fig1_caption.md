**Figure 1. Flow of records from retrieval to the 2021-2025 analytic corpus, with exclusions and human validation samples.** Boxes on the left follow the JARS stages (identified, screened, assessed for eligibility, included in analysis) applied to bibliographic records; boxes in the middle give each exclusion with its reason and n. The dashed box marks the R1 analysis window (2021-2025), which replaces the April 2026 cover-date cutoff; January-April 2026 records (n=10,757) appear only in a descriptive supplementary table. The estimate of AI-engaged records among those excluded by the prefilter comes from the R1 prefilter audit (design-weighted 1-NPV, Jeffreys interval; Multimedia Appendix 3). The analytic subsets show the denominators for the theme, mechanism, specialty and geography analyses. Critical stance was Alarm or Caution. The right-hand column lists every human validation sample with its n and design; agreement statistics are in Table 1. Q: SCImago Journal Rank quartile; PMID: PubMed identifier.
