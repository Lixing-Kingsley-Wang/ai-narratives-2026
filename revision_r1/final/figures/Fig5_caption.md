**Figure 5. Critical stance by country of first authorship, 2021-2025.** The map shows the 32 countries with at least 50 first-author records among 12,745 records whose first-author affiliation resolved to a country. Bubble area is proportional to the number of records; fill colour shows the critical rate (centred on the corpus rate of 30.5%), and the printed value is the critical rate (%). The boxed area of Europe is enlarged in the inset, where its values are printed. Country was assigned from the first-author affiliation and indexes publication discourse, not national attitudes, research quality or AI capability. Counts, 95% percentile bootstrap CIs (1000 resamples within country, seed 42) and the 20-record map are in Multimedia Appendices 2 and 3. Critical stance was Alarm or Caution. Robinson projection.
